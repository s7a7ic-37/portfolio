Stockroom Reconcile

Review a stock count

Required columns: sku, expected, counted. Optional columns: name, condition. Condition can be sellable, damaged, or held.

Quantities must be whole, non-negative numbers. Repeated SKUs are held for review instead of being combined.

    On a narrow screen, swipe the table sideways to see every column.
